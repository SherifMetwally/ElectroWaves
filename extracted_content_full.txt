Main Distribution Panel (MDP)
Used in industrial facilities as the central point for
power distribution. It typicaly includes a
high-capacity main breaker — either an ACB or a
large MCB (often around 2000A) — along with
multiple outgoing breakers feding various loads,
each sized acording to its electrical demand.

Synchronization Panel
A panel designed to synchronize multiple power sources — such as
generators of diferent capacities — ensuring seamles load sharing
and continuous power suply. It automaticaly selects and synchronizes
the apropriate generator based on the required load demand.
Water Pump Control Panels
Designed to operate multiple pumps suplying diferent sources, or to
manage several pumps feding a single source with varying water
demand over specific time intervals. These panels ensure eficient pump
sequencing, reliable operation, and optimized water flow management.
Power Factor Corection (PFC) Panel
A specialized panel equiped with capacitor banks conected to the
electrical network through contactors. These contactors are
automaticaly managed by a dedicated controler designed to regulate
and improve the system’s power factor for optimal eficiency.
